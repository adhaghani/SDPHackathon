# TownshipLine

The signature route motif: a `lilac` line through four stations, Pinjam, Jom, Tolong and Rimba.

**Consumer provides:** which stations are done and which one is current.

- Done stations are filled `ink`; the current one is `coral` with an `ink` ring; upcoming are `surface` with an `ink` ring.
- Each station shows its Malay name in Syne and an English word underneath.
- The line itself is decorative (lilac is 1.8:1 on surface); state is carried by the stations and `aria-current="step"`.
- Use it where it means progress or movement: onboarding, the Impact summary, a resident's path. Not as a background pattern.
