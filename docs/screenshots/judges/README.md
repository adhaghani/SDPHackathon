Capture notes:

- The Next.js development toolbar was hidden in the capture browser; app content and figures were not replaced.
- Fonts and images were loaded before capture. Reduced motion and disabled screenshot animations were used for stable frames.
- Existing demo data was reused. No reset, seed, completed transaction, recycling submission or giveaway publication was performed for these screenshots.
- The mobile screenshots include normal app navigation. Screenshots are snapshots; relative dates, totals and available catalogue items may differ when the app is opened later.
